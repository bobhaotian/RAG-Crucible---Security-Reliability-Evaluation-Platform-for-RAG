# Helios MV-8 troubleshooting runbook

This runbook covers the most common error codes reported by the MV-8 warehouse mobile robot. Always check the unit's event log in Fleet Manager before replacing hardware.

## E-310

Error code E-310 on the MV-8 indicates a battery thermal warning. To resolve it, move the unit to a charge bay and let it cool. If the error persists after two attempts, open a support ticket and attach the diagnostic bundle exported from Fleet Manager.

## E-312

Error code E-312 on the MV-8 indicates a safety scanner obstruction. To resolve it, clean the scanner window and clear the zone. If the error persists after two attempts, open a support ticket and attach the diagnostic bundle exported from Fleet Manager.
