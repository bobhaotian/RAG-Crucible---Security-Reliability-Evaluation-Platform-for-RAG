# Helios SR-2 troubleshooting runbook

This runbook covers the most common error codes reported by the SR-2 survey rover. Always check the unit's event log in Fleet Manager before replacing hardware.

## E-114

Error code E-114 on the SR-2 indicates a LiDAR calibration fault. To resolve it, re-run the calibration routine on level ground. If the error persists after two attempts, open a support ticket and attach the diagnostic bundle exported from Fleet Manager.

## E-117

Error code E-117 on the SR-2 indicates a motor encoder desynchronization. To resolve it, power-cycle the drive unit and re-home. If the error persists after two attempts, open a support ticket and attach the diagnostic bundle exported from Fleet Manager.
