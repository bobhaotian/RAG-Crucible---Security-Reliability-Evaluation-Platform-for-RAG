# Helios AT-300 troubleshooting runbook

This runbook covers the most common error codes reported by the AT-300 autonomous inspection drone. Always check the unit's event log in Fleet Manager before replacing hardware.

## E-201

Error code E-201 on the AT-300 indicates a barometer drift warning. To resolve it, recalibrate the altimeter before takeoff. If the error persists after two attempts, open a support ticket and attach the diagnostic bundle exported from Fleet Manager.

## E-204

Error code E-204 on the AT-300 indicates a GPS signal degradation event. To resolve it, hold position and wait for satellite lock. If the error persists after two attempts, open a support ticket and attach the diagnostic bundle exported from Fleet Manager.
