# Helios SR-2 specification

The Helios SR-2 is a survey rover designed for ground-level mapping and surveying of construction sites and mines. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The SR-2 has a battery life of 28 hours. A full charge of the SR-2 takes 165 minutes.
The SR-2 carries a maximum payload of 10 kg. The SR-2 weighs 100 kg.
The SR-2 reaches a top speed of 1.5 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the SR-2 is 2.6.4. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The SR-2 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The SR-2 is priced at 49,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
