# Helios MV-8 specification

The Helios MV-8 is a warehouse mobile robot designed for autonomous pallet transport inside fulfillment centers. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The MV-8 has a battery life of 10 hours. A full charge of the MV-8 takes 90 minutes.
The MV-8 carries a maximum payload of 15 kg. The MV-8 weighs 226 kg.
The MV-8 reaches a top speed of 2.6 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the MV-8 is 5.5.13. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The MV-8 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The MV-8 is priced at 82,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
