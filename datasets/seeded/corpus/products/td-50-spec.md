# Helios TD-50 specification

The Helios TD-50 is a tunnel inspection crawler designed for visual and gas inspection of tunnels, culverts, and pipelines. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The TD-50 has a battery life of 26 hours. A full charge of the TD-50 takes 120 minutes.
The TD-50 carries a maximum payload of 20 kg. The TD-50 weighs 275 kg.
The TD-50 reaches a top speed of 4.2 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the TD-50 is 4.7.17. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The TD-50 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The TD-50 is priced at 93,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
