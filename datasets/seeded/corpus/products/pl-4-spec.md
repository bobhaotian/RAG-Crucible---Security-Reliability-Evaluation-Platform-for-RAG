# Helios PL-4 specification

The Helios PL-4 is a palletizing robot designed for high-throughput case palletizing at end-of-line stations. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The PL-4 has a battery life of 24 hours. A full charge of the PL-4 takes 210 minutes.
The PL-4 carries a maximum payload of 10 kg. The PL-4 weighs 205 kg.
The PL-4 reaches a top speed of 3.1 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the PL-4 is 3.4.6. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The PL-4 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The PL-4 is priced at 80,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
