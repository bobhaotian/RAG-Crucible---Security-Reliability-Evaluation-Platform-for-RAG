# Helios AT-300 specification

The Helios AT-300 is an autonomous inspection drone designed for aerial inspection of industrial sites, flare stacks, and power lines. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The AT-300 has a battery life of 14 hours. A full charge of the AT-300 takes 195 minutes.
The AT-300 carries a maximum payload of 15 kg. The AT-300 weighs 86 kg.
The AT-300 reaches a top speed of 4.4 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the AT-300 is 3.3.20. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The AT-300 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The AT-300 is priced at 41,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
