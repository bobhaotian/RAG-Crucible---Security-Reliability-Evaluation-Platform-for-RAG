# Helios CB-2 specification

The Helios CB-2 is a conveyor inspection bot designed for rail-mounted monitoring of long conveyor systems. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The CB-2 has a battery life of 30 hours. A full charge of the CB-2 takes 75 minutes.
The CB-2 carries a maximum payload of 40 kg. The CB-2 weighs 191 kg.
The CB-2 reaches a top speed of 4.1 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the CB-2 is 4.3.20. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The CB-2 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The CB-2 is priced at 44,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
