# Helios HX-12 specification

The Helios HX-12 is a robotic arm designed for precision pick-and-place and light assembly on production lines. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The HX-12 has a battery life of 32 hours. A full charge of the HX-12 takes 105 minutes.
The HX-12 carries a maximum payload of 120 kg. The HX-12 weighs 72 kg.
The HX-12 reaches a top speed of 6.0 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the HX-12 is 4.7.13. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The HX-12 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The HX-12 is priced at 22,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
