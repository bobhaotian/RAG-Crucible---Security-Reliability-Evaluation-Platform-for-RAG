# Helios GS-9 specification

The Helios GS-9 is a gas detection station designed for fixed-position monitoring of combustible and toxic gases. It is part of the
Helios Robotics industrial automation line and integrates with the Helios Fleet Manager
for scheduling, telemetry, and over-the-air updates.

## Specifications

The GS-9 has a battery life of 10 hours. A full charge of the GS-9 takes 120 minutes.
The GS-9 carries a maximum payload of 60 kg. The GS-9 weighs 212 kg.
The GS-9 reaches a top speed of 2.7 m/s. The drive system uses brushless motors with regenerative braking.

## Software

The current firmware version for the GS-9 is 4.6.4. Firmware updates are delivered over the air through Fleet Manager and
can be staged to a test group before fleet-wide rollout. The GS-9 exposes a
local REST interface for diagnostics and supports MQTT for telemetry streaming.

## Pricing and availability

The GS-9 is priced at 45,000 USD. Volume discounts apply to orders of ten or more units. Standard lead time
is six weeks from purchase order.
