# Vacation policy

Full-time employees receive 23 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation accrues monthly and unused days roll over up to a cap of ten
days. Requests go through the HR portal and need manager approval at least two weeks in
advance for absences longer than three days.

Employees also receive 12 paid sick days per year, which do not roll over.
Public holidays follow the provincial calendar of each employee's home office.
