Frequently asked questions

Warranty

The standard warranty period is 36 months.

Extended coverage of up to five years can be purchased per unit at order time.

Support

Support is available from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern Time on weekdays.

Enterprise plans include a 24/7 emergency line for production-down incidents.

Spare parts

Spare parts ship from regional depots and arrive within three business days for standard orders.
